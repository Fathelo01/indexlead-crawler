Our crawler: IndexLeadBot

If you found IndexLeadBot in your server logs, this page explains who we are, what it does and how to stop it.

Who runs it

Fabrice Thenor Louis runs IndexLead, a service that helps businesses find other businesses to contact. You can reach us at indexlead.crawler@outlook.com.

What it does

For UK limited companies listed on the public Companies House register, it looks for the company's own website and reads a few public pages: the home page and, if they exist, the contact, about, terms and privacy pages. It does two things with them:

  • It checks that the site really belongs to the company, by looking for the company's registered number (UK companies must show it on their website).
  • It notes business email addresses on your own domain that the site publishes (such as info@ or a named address).

It does not log in, fill in forms, read pages that need a password, or follow links to other websites.

How it behaves

  • It identifies itself: IndexLeadBot/1.0 (+this page; a contact address).
  • It reads your robots.txt first and obeys it. If it cannot read that file, it does not crawl the site.
  • It makes at most one request to a site every two seconds, and reads only a handful of pages per company.
  • It connects only to public internet addresses and reads at most half a megabyte per page.

How to stop it

Add this to your robots.txt and it will not visit your site again:

User-agent: IndexLeadBot
Disallow: /

To have what we already hold removed, write to indexlead.crawler@outlook.com with your website address, company number or email address. Anyone named in our data can also ask to see it, correct it or have it deleted, and a request to stop all contact is kept on a list so it is never added again.
